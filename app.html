Dice Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts the outcomes of perfectly fair, independent dice and tries. It does not know whether any real die, game or machine is fair, and it says nothing about odds you are offered or about whether to play. Every figure is your own entry.

The sum truth

How many of the equally likely rolls give a total.

The edge truth

One die needing a number or higher, with a single roll, advantage and disadvantage.

The streak truth

A 1 in X chance tried over and over.
